The language phrase no longer comes back on a cycle. Themes rotate every eighteen days while the anti-repeat window was thirty, so a theme's first phrase had aged out by the time the theme came round again, and the same phrase was taught again 36 to 90 days later. The writer now sees the last 90 days of phrases (`history_days`) plus every phrase ever taught on today's theme; the history file keeps a year, each entry records its theme, and a phrase that repeats an earlier one is noted in the log.
