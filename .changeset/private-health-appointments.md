Health appointments reach the brief as "Private appointment". A calendar event whose title names a clinician, a credential, a specialty, a procedure or a place of care, or whose location is a clinic or hospital, loses its title, place and notes before the writer sees it and carries `health: true`, so the printed page shows "Appointment" with the person and the time. The writer had been told never to print those details and still did. Visits to the vet are left alone, and the new `private_patterns` calendar option covers practices the built-in vocabulary cannot recognise.
