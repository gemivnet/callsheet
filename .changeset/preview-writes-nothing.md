A preview (`--preview` or the dashboard's generate) no longer closes Todoist tasks or rewrites the language phrase history. Auto-close ran on previews too, so regenerating a brief to look at it could close tasks, and the preview's phrase replaced the one the morning's brief had printed, letting that phrase come back later. Both now happen only on real runs, like the task coach and the living context.
